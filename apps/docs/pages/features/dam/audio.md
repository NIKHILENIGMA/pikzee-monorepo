# 🎵 Audio Asset Management

Audio tracks (MP3, WAV, AAC) are supported for inclusion in document attachments or social posting video overlays.

---

## 1. Storage & Verification

Audio files are verified against duration limits before saving. The database records file size, sample rate, and compression codecs for search filtering.

---

## 2. Playback Previews

The UI renders a custom audio player card featuring:

- Waveform visualizations (pre-rendered or generated at runtime).
- Play/Pause, volume, and playback speed adjustments.
